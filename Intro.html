My Culture

I am a Muganda from Uganda, a country in East Africa. The Baganda are one of the largest ethnic groups in Uganda. Their traditional language is Luganda, and their kingdom is called Buganda. Baganda culture includes traditional music, dances, clothing, food, stories, customs and proverbs. Elders help pass cultural knowledge and traditions from one generation to another.
