My Culture - Baganda

I am a Muganda from Uganda, a country in East Africa. The Baganda are one of the largest ethnic groups in Uganda. Their traditional language is Luganda, and their kingdom is called Buganda.

Baganda culture has many traditions and customs. These include traditional music, dances, clothing, food, ceremonies and storytelling. The elders play an important role in teaching younger generations about their history and traditions.

Oral traditions are also important in Baganda culture. Legends, myths and proverbs are passed down through generations. They help preserve cultural knowledge and teach people important lessons about life.
